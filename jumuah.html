Salat-ul-Jumu’ah

Two Jama’ats are held for Jumu’ah each week. No pre-booking is required.

Jumu’ah Procedure

  1. Bayaan / Waaz — normally in Bengali during the first session, and in English during the second session.
  2. First Adhaan, followed by a short break for the Sunnats (Sunan).
  3. Second Adhaan, Jumu’ah Khutbah, and Salaah in congregation — Salaah begins approximately 10 minutes after the second Adhaan.
