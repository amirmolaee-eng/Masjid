Etiquette of the Masjid

A few reminders to help us all keep the Masjid a place of peace, focus and respect.

  1. Perform Wudhu and go to the Masjid with the intention of Salaat. All the time spent waiting for Salaat with Jama’at to commence is awarded as though you were in Salaat — and the Malaaikah make Du’aa for the one who waits in the Masjid.
  2. If it is not Makrooh time, perform two Rakaats Tahiyyatul Masjid before sitting down. Read the third Kalimah instead if it is Makrooh time.
  3. Do not raise your voice in the Masjid.
  4. Avoid talking of worldly affairs — this destroys one’s rewards just as flames consume dry wood.
  5. Never pass in front of a person performing Salaat.
  6. Do not argue over a particular place in the Masjid.
  7. Avoid spitting or blowing your nose in the Masjid.
  8. Keep the body Taahir (Paak) at all times.
  9. Keep yourself occupied with the remembrance of Allah.
  10. If you have lost something, it should not be announced in the Masjid.
  11. Food with a strong odour should not be eaten before coming to the Masjid. Smokers should be mindful of their offensive odour and the discomfort it causes others — the Malaaikah are annoyed by the same things that annoy human beings.
  12. If your clothing has an offensive smell (e.g. from excessive sweating), remove the smell or change the clothing before entering the Masjid.
  13. Dhikr and Qur’an recital should be made in a manner that does not disturb others.
  14. Avoid cracking your fingers.
  15. Sit respectfully in the Masjid, without stretching your legs towards the Qibla.
  16. Avoid any act that distracts others.

Source: Al-Jamiat Publications.
