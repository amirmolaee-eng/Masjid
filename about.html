About Us

Serving the Muslim community of north Cambridge since our founding as a registered charity.

Omar Faruque Mosque and Cultural Centre is a not-for-profit organisation run by a group of trustees and a management committee. We are a UK registered charity, No. 1117040, with an established and approved constitution, and we exist to look after the needs of the Muslim community in the north of Cambridge.

The Mosque (Masjid) is located in the Arbury area of Cambridge, close to Cambridge Science Park and Cambridge Regional College, and serves as a place of worship for Muslims to come together for Salah (prayer) and to learn more about Islam.

Our congregation is wonderfully diverse, comprising people from many different backgrounds and nationalities — local residents and students alike.

Our Imam

The Imam of the Mosque is Mawlānā Mohammed Abdul Gani, MM (Momtazul Muhaddisin), MF (Momtazul Fuqaha), MA National University of Bangladesh.

His spiritual knowledge comes from Shaykh Allamah Abdul Latif Chowdhury Saheb Qiblah Fultali (Rahmatullahi Alaihi) of the noble Jaunpuri Silsilāh (spiritual chain) of Tariqāh-e Naqshbandiyah wa Mujaddidiyah wa Muhammadiyah.
