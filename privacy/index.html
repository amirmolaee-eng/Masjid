Privacy & Cookies

How this website handles your information. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Omar Faruque Mosque and Cultural Centre, a registered charity in England and Wales (no. 1117040), Kirkwood Road, Cambridge, CB4 2PF. We are the “data controller” for any personal information described on this page.

What this website collects

Our website does not ask you to create an account, does not use advertising or analytics tools, and does not currently collect personal information through a form. The contact form on the Contact page is not yet active.

Like most websites, the company that hosts it may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address, date and time, and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

If you contact the mosque by phone, email or in person, we will use the details you give us only to reply to you and to run the mosque’s activities.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set any cookies on this website, and the fonts we use are stored on our own site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts. Because we only use technology that is strictly necessary, we do not show a cookie banner.

The Location page can show a Google map. It stays switched off until you choose “Show map”. If you do, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies under Google’s own privacy policy. You can use the “Open in Google Maps” link or Apple Maps instead.

Links to other services

Our pages link to other organisations, including Square (for online donations), Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, Google Maps and Apple Maps. When you follow those links you leave this website, and the other organisation’s privacy policy applies. We do not receive your payment card details when you donate through Square.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. To do so, please use the Contact page. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we add features that collect personal information (such as a contact form, event sign-up or newsletter), we will update this page first.
